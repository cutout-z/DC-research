Tenant credit

Australian operators

NEXTDC, Goodman, Macquarie Technology, AirTrunk, CDC, Equinix and the other owners and operators of Australian data centres, seen as a market: the companies that borrow against long-dated tenant leases. An operator's leverage, its funding cadence and the tenants its capacity is contracted to set how much lease income can fall before debt service is strained. The node follows them as market participants, through listed financials and announced raises, debt facilities and contracts.

How it is measured
Formula
Median, across the listed operators, of debt to equity: total debt ÷ shareholders' equity, in %, from each one's latest reported balance sheet, as the AU lane's Yahoo Finance quotes carry it (NXT, GMG and MAQ at present; others as they list). Per operator with it: EV/EBITDA and market capitalisation. As of: the newest date in financials_history.parquet, written in the same run as the quotes, which carry no date, so it dates the prices: stale after 21 days. Also stale when the oldest operator's balance sheet (most_recent_quarter, the period end the ratio comes from) is over 253 days old: a half-year plus the ASX's two months to report it, plus a week. Net debt ÷ EBITDA and capex ÷ operating cash flow are not in these files, so they are not computed. Change: the same median of the quotes as they stood 3 months before as_of (else 1 month), from their recorded history.
Inputs
data/au_dc/processed/financials_quotes.parquet: ticker, name, debt_to_equity, ev_ebitda, market_cap, most_recent_quarter
data/au_dc/processed/financials_history.parquet: date
data/history/au_quotes/index.csv: vintage_date, file
Refresh
AU lane: weekly quotes; ratios move with semi-annual statements · owner AU lane · stale after 21 days
Notes
The median of 3 ASX-listed operators' debt to equity (GMG, MAQ, NXT), each from its latest reported balance sheet, dated 19 Oct 2026 by the newest week of the price history. Balance sheets: the oldest is dated 30 Jun 2026; the reading is stale once it is over 253 days old. Net debt ÷ EBITDA and capex ÷ operating cash flow are not in the AU lane's files. Goodman's ratio is the whole group's, most of it industrial property. Earlier values are the quotes file as it stood on each date, from its recorded history (data/history/au_quotes, 7 versions since 13 Apr 2026), corrections included: a change can be a market move or a fix to the data.
