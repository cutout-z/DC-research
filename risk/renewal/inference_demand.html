Inference demand

How much AI work is actually being run: token volume, and consumer and enterprise adoption of AI services. AI workloads in Australian data centres are inference and cloud rather than training, so this is the demand a renewal depends on: a tenant renews capacity that paying workloads still fill. The dashboard has no measure of it; frontier-lab revenue is related reading, not a measure of token volume.
