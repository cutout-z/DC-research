How the check works

  • When. Weekly, as a step of the dashboard's ETL refresh (Saturday morning, Perth time), after the guidance, consensus, lease and news files are refreshed. The public site picks the result up at its next rebuild.
  • Filed figures. Each XBRL-tagged figure is read again from SEC EDGAR companyfacts for the same concept and period; the newest filing wins. A difference of more than $1m and 0.05% is a restatement (moved).
  • Latest period and filings. EDGAR's filing index for each company: a 10-K, 10-Q or 20-F for a later period, or an 8-K results release (Item 2.02), supersedes the model's latest period; a 6-K is a filing to read.
  • Guidance, consensus and leases. Compared with the dashboard's own capex guidance file (moved beyond 0.5%), analyst consensus file (moved beyond 3%), and leases-not-yet-commenced file. Guidance for a period that has ended is superseded by filed actuals.
  • Useful lives. The model's asset lives are assumptions. Filings since the inputs that discuss server or data-centre useful lives (EDGAR full-text search) are listed to read for a change in stated lives.
  • News. Headlines from the dashboard's news catalogue published after an input's date and naming the company and a relevant topic. They are leads to a filing or company statement, never figures. ZeroHedge items are excluded, and paywalled articles appear only as their public headline.
  • Nothing is changed automatically. The model is rebuilt from its sources in a separate, reviewed step.
